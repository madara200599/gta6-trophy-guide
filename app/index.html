          How this guide is built

          • Trophy names, descriptions and grades are shown exactly as PlayStation publishes them.
          • Every guide is written for this entry, in its own words. A source is cited, never copied.
          • A guide gives no credit to a location or step it cannot cite — where no published walkthrough pins one down yet, the guide says so.
          • Progress is saved in your browser (localStorage) and never leaves your machine. Export and import are local files.
          • This app works with the network off and fetches nothing at run time. No Rockstar logo, artwork or game screenshot is used.
